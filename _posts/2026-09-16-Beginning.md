Blue<br>

It used to be that all my sight<br>
Was filled with shades of black and white.<br>
Then, one day, I found someone<br>
And somehow I could see alright.<br>

I'd felt so small and had no clue<br>
That everything could have such hue,<br>
But when you came and held my hand<br>
I felt like you had changed my view.<br>

A rainbow of colors sprouted out<br>
From the trust you showed me without a doubt.<br>
My struggles before all faded away<br>
When I found what understanding's all about.<br>

Whenever I feel like I'm losing my way<br>
And my will starts to shake and shiver and sway,<br>
I'll still stand and fight and look to the light,<br>
'Cause when I have you I won't go astray.<br>

Sometimes the sunlight is hidden from view<br>
And shadows will threaten to drown me anew.<br>
But I'll start to smile when we talk awhile,<br>
'Cause when I'm with you, the skies are blue.
